| D-27 | Invalid preferences on draft save | A draft save with invalid preferences is refused with `422`; nothing is saved. Other incomplete fields remain allowed in a draft. | 2026-11-11 |
| D-15 | Submission snapshot | Each submitted application keeps a copy of the candidate's personal data as submitted. Later edits or ICP refreshes do not change it. | 2026-11-11 |
