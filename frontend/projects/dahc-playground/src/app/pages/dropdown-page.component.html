Items: buttons, a link ("Help centre"), a disabled action ("Appeal score", skipped by the arrow keys) and a destructive action (error colour).

Read the guide
  • The trigger gets aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls; Enter, Space or ArrowDown opens on the first item, ArrowUp on the last.
  • In the menu: ArrowUp / ArrowDown / Home / End move, Enter or Space choose, Escape closes back to the trigger, Tab closes.
