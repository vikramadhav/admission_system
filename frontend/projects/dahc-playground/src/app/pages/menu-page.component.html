Items: with icons, disabled ("Appeal score") and destructive ("Withdraw application", error colour).

  • The button reports aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; Enter, Space or ArrowDown opens on the first item, ArrowUp on the last.
  • In the menu: ArrowUp / ArrowDown / Home / End move, Enter or Space choose, Escape closes back to the button, Tab closes and moves on.
  • Disabled items stay in the list for discoverability but are skipped by the arrow keys.
