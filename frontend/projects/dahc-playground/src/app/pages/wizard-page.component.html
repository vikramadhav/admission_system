Form fields of this step.

Complete (navy, tick), current (blue row), upcoming (grey), error (red). Sub-steps show under the current step.

  • The stepper is a labelled nav with an ordered list; the current step and sub-step have aria-current="step".
  • Completed, current and error steps are buttons for going back; upcoming steps are plain text.
  • Every step announces its status ("Completed", "Current step", "Not started", "Needs attention").
  • The action bar is a labelled group and stays in reach (sticky) in an application.
